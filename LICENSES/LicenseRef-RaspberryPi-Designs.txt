Raspberry Pi design files

Raspberry Pi Ltd publishes the design files of the Compute Module 5 IO Board (KiCad, revision 2):
https://pip-assets.raspberrypi.com/categories/1098-design-files/documents/RP-008099-DD-1-CM5%20IO%20Board,%20revision%202,%20KiCAD%20files..zip

The archive carries no licence file. Raspberry Pi's licensing page
(https://www.raspberrypi.com/licensing/) grants, for the design files it hosts, "permission to
use, copy, modify, and/or distribute these designs for any purpose". Third-party parts in the
archive (its 3D models) remain under their makers' terms and are not included in this repository.

The authoritative terms are those on Raspberry Pi's licensing page; check it before redistributing.
